What this app does

This app reminds you to take medications and to do planned activities, and records whether you did. It is a reminder tool and does not give medical advice.

Data stored on your device

Your medications, schedules, dose history, stock counts, reminders and settings are stored only on your device, encrypted by iOS. They are not sent to us unless you turn on sharing.

Data stored in our cloud (only if you create an account)

  • Your name, email address and User ID, so your account works.
  • Your date of birth, only if you choose to add it.
  • Which version of this policy and the Terms you accepted, and when.
  • Your country or region.

Sharing with family (optional)

If you accept a request from someone to follow your progress, a copy of your medication list, schedules, the past year of dose history and your streak is stored in our cloud so that person can see it. You choose who, and you can stop sharing at any time; stopping removes the cloud copy.

Age

Accounts are for people 18 and older; we ask for your date of birth before an account is created and record only that you confirmed (your date of birth is stored only if you choose to add it). We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Guest mode keeps everything on the device and sends us nothing, so it can be used at any age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Emails

We don't send marketing emails. Sign-in uses Apple or Google, so we don't send sign-in emails either.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We receive the subscription status, never your payment details.

What we never do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or third-party analytics.

Your choices

You can export or delete your data, and delete your account from Profile → Account. Deleting your account removes your cloud data and the data on this device.

Contact

Questions? Use Contact support in the app.
