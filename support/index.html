Common questions

Reminders not arriving? Check that notifications are on in Profile → Notifications, and that Focus or Low Power Mode isn't silencing them.

Cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DoseDay → Cancel. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.

Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple; request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your account and data: Profile → Account → Delete account. Guests can use Profile → Erase data on this device.
