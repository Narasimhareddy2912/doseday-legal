Delete your Dosely account

Applies to the Dosely app on Android phones and tablets. Deleting is permanent.

Delete in the app (fastest)

Open Dosely → Profile → Account → Delete account, turn on I understand this can't be undone and confirm. It happens immediately.

Request deletion without the app

Email doselyquery@gmail.com from the email address of your account, and include your 5-character User ID if you know it. To protect you, we first send a confirmation to that address; once you confirm, we delete the account, usually within 7 days, and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, User ID and date of birth (if added)
  • Any medication information you shared with family, and all family connections (the other people are told that sharing ended)
  • Push-notification registrations and subscription records

What is kept

Nothing in our cloud. Medications, dose history and reminders stored on your phone stay there until you delete the app or use Erase data on this device. Store purchase records are kept by the store under its own policies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
